## Script

The first sentence is the §1 identity line, word for word. No market context, no "imagine a world" before it.

Default 3 minutes:

| Part | Time | What |
|------|------|------|
| Meet the user | 30s | §13 who: one person, one moment, their pain. End on the §3 impact number. |
| Live journey | 90s | The §9 demo path on the live URL. The wow moment lands in the middle, with the §13 wow line. |
| Why it could ship | 30s | The §4 hard part line and one real number from `docs/evidence.md`. |
| What's next | 30s | §13 what's next. One sentence, then stop. |

For other lengths, scale each part. Keep the problem near 30% of the time. Mark each part with its time, and write the words to say, not notes.

## Q&A bank

One honest sentence each:

- Who exactly is this for?
- What did you build during the event?
- What breaks first at scale?
- What does it cost per user?
- Where does the data come from, and do you have permission to use it?
- Which model do you use, and how do you check its output? (`ai.md` AI-10)

If the honest answer is weak, say so in the file. Don't dress it up.

## Jargon check

List every term a non-specialist judge wouldn't know, with a plain replacement. Use the replacements in the script.

## Outsider test

End your reply by telling me: read the first 30 seconds to someone who hasn't seen the project, and have them say it back. If they can't repeat the identity line, rewrite §1 before the next dry run.
